Instructions

Welcome to the rabbit study tool!
There are a few basic rules to the tool.

Navigation

There are 6 main pages - each of the pages are connected to the other pages they relate to.
The homepage is connected to all pages, so if you get lost, just go back there!

Pages

The homepage allows navigation to all other pages.

Tasks

In the tasks page, you will be able to input 3 tasks, and then head over to the adjustable timer page to complete the study tasks to get coins. Once you have completed a task, you click complete on the task to get a reward of 2 coins.

Shop

These coins can be spent on buying food from the shop page - food bought goes to the interact feeding section.

Timer

The timer page has an adjustable pomodoro timer - you can put in anywhere from 1 minute to 90 minutes of solid study for a session, and your break time will automatically adjust. The longer you study, the more coins and happiness you get! Remember, you have to study for a minimum of 5 minutes to get rewards!

Interact page

The final page is the interact page - you can feed your rabbit with food you've bought. This increases their happiness! Different food gives different levels of happiness. You can also pat your rabbit to get +15 happiness every 3 hours.

Warnings

If you let your rabbit's happiness drop too much, they can become sick. Remember to study often to prevent this!
(The longer you go without studying using the timer, the more the happiness meter decreases.)
Please don't enter any personal information such as the users real name as the rabbit's name!
This is a trust-based tool - the one benefiting is you, so please don't just tick off tasks if they haven't been finished!

Your Rabbit

Your rabbit relies on you and you alone. Remember, studying won't just keep your rabbit healthy, it will also make life less stressful for you!

Other information

You can rename your rabbit in the text box above its head on the homepage!
